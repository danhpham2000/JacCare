# Demo Script

## Opening

Many people delay healthcare not because care does not exist, but because the path to affordable care is confusing.

## Product

CareRoute AI turns local clinics, pharmacies, transit routes, city services, eligibility rules, and cost options into a graph. Jac walkers traverse that graph to find the lowest-cost, most realistic care path.

## Live Demo

Use the preset scenario:

Uninsured parent, $50 budget, no car, Spanish preferred, child has fever.

Show:

1. Intake fields
2. Safety check
3. Recommended clinic: Community Health Access Clinic
4. Backup clinic: Eastside Family Care
5. Cost estimate and score breakdown
6. Bus Route 7 transportation
7. Document checklist
8. CHIP and city support programs
9. Prescription savings
10. Call script and Spanish translation
11. Jac match graph

## Jac Explanation

The graph contains users, clinics, pharmacies, city services, eligibility rules, cost options, and transit routes. Jac walkers handle intake, safety checks, eligibility matching, care matching, transport routing, cost ranking, and plan generation.

## Closing

CareRoute AI helps people find care before small problems become expensive emergencies.
