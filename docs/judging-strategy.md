# Judging Strategy

## Best Use of Jac

Lead with the graph. Show the Jac node and walker declarations in `backend/main.jac`, then show the graph visualization in the UI. Explain that the user is not just searching a list; walkers traverse connected care resources and return a ranked plan.

## Consumer Healthcare

Emphasize that CareRoute AI does not diagnose. It handles access questions: where to go, what it may cost, what to bring, how to get there, and what to ask when calling.

## Social Impact

Show CHIP support, transit vouchers, food support, immigrant resource navigation, and prescription assistance alongside the clinic result.

## Fintech

Discuss cost optimization: visit estimates, sliding-scale support, payment-plan flags, pharmacy discount options, and budget fit.

## Agentic AI

Explain the safety-first orchestration: deterministic red flags, graph matching, eligibility scoring, cost ranking, and then AI-safe plan text or translation.

## Startup Path

Potential customers include city health departments, counties, community clinics, nonprofits, universities, Medicaid support organizations, and hospital outreach teams.
